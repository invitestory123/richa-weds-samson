# Richa & Samson — Wedding Invitation

A digital royal wedding invitation experience celebrating the wedding of **Richa Arora** & **Samson**.

- **Dates**: 13th & 14th December 2026
- **Venue**: Kaka Ji Ni Wadi, Panvel, Navi Mumbai
- **Functions**:
  - 13 Dec 11:00 AM: Carnival (Haldi & Mehendi) @ Pool Side (Dark Pink, Yellow & Orange)
  - 13 Dec 7:00 PM: Engagement & Sangeet / Cocktail @ Sagar Palace Banquet (Red & Dark Blue)
  - 14 Dec 4:00 PM: The Sacred Pheras @ The Lawn (Anything but Red / Maroon)
  - 14 Dec 7:00 PM: Grand Reception @ The Lawn (Anything but Red / Maroon)
- **Soundtrack**: Chaap Tilak (Instrumental) – Mangesh Jagtap
- **Assets**: Lossless 1200x630 Open Graph Image (`/og-image.png`)

## Development

```sh
npm run dev
```
